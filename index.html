Bright News Shield

Search now follows your selected comfort level too. Uplifting searches specifically for positive developments and requires a constructive signal; Gentle allows positive and neutral developments; Need-to-Know is intentionally broader. Crime, lockdowns, weapons, arrests, tragedy and fear-heavy headlines remain blocked from the gentler views. Publisher pages are outside Bright News, so they can still contain unrelated headlines or imagery.

Bright News is a filtered news reader, not an emergency-alert service. Automated filtering can miss context, so use Need-to-Know when you want a broader view.
