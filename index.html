Bright News Shield

Before a headline reaches this screen, Bright News filters terms associated with graphic violence, tragedy, disasters, outrage-heavy politics and other distressing subjects. The filter is intentionally conservative, so it may occasionally hide a perfectly safe story.

Bright News uses GDELT’s live news index to discover current publisher links. Filtering is automated and cannot guarantee that an outside article page will be free of upsetting material. Bright News is not an emergency-alert service.
